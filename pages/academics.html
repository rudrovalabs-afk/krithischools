CBSE learning, built on strong foundations

From the first days of Pre-KG to the Class 10 board year, our English-medium programme grows with every child — concept by concept, skill by skill.

Our Approach

Learning that makes sense to children

We follow the CBSE framework with an activity-first philosophy — children understand ideas, not just memorise them.

Concept-first

Lessons start from real examples and hands-on activities, so understanding is deep and lasting.

English Medium

A strong focus on reading, speaking and writing builds confident communicators.

Continuous Assessment

Regular, friendly assessments track progress and guide each child forward without fear.

Stages of Learning

A programme for every age

Pre-KG · LKG · UKG

Pre-Primary

A gentle, playful start where little ones learn through stories, songs, colours and games.

  • Language, phonics & early numbers
  • Motor skills, rhymes, art & play
  • Good habits, sharing & confidence
Classes 1 – 5

Primary

Building blocks of literacy, numeracy and scientific temper, taught with plenty of activities.

  • English, Mathematics, EVS & Science
  • Second & third language, Computers
  • Art, music, sports & value education
Classes 6 – 8

Middle School

Concepts deepen and thinking sharpens as students explore subjects in greater detail.

  • Science, Mathematics & Social Science
  • Languages, Computers & projects
  • Clubs, debates & co-curricular growth
Classes 9 – 10

Secondary

Focused preparation for the CBSE board examinations, with mentoring and steady practice.

  • Board-aligned teaching & revision
  • Regular tests, doubt-clearing & guidance
  • Career awareness & personal mentoring
