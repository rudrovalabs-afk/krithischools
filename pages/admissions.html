Documents Required

What to keep ready

Please carry the following at the time of admission (originals for verification and one photocopy each).

  • Child's birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card of child & parents
  • Previous school's Transfer Certificate (for Class 1 and above)
  • Previous year's report card / marks memo
  • Recent passport-size photographs (4)

Note: document requirements are indicative. Our office will confirm the exact list for your child's class at the time of enquiry.
