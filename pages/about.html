Our Story

A small school with a big heart

Krithi International School opened its doors in 2012 with a simple promise to the families of Kavuru and Chilakaluripet — quality, values-based education close to home.

What began as a close-knit community of teachers and children has grown year after year, yet our spirit remains the same. We are proud to be a place where teachers know every child by name, where learning is joyful, and where discipline and kindness go hand in hand. Located on Guntur Main Road, we follow the CBSE curriculum in English medium for both boys and girls, from Pre-KG right through to Class 10.

Our Vision

To be a trusted school that shapes confident, compassionate and curious young citizens — children who carry their inner light into the world, just as our motto inspires.

Our Mission

To deliver strong CBSE academics with individual attention, in a safe and caring environment that balances books, values, play and creativity for every child.
