Welcome to Krithi

A caring home for curious young minds

Set along Guntur Main Road in Kavuru, Chilakaluripet, Krithi International School blends the academic rigour of the CBSE curriculum with the warmth of individual attention. We believe every child carries a unique light — our role is to help it shine.

  • Activity-based, concept-first CBSE teaching
  • Safe, disciplined and joyful environment
  • Values rooted in Indian culture & curiosity
